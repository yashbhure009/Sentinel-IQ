# SentinelIQ — Legal & Privacy

## Data handling

SentinelIQ is a self-hosted tool. When you run it:
- All scanning happens on YOUR machine
- All findings are written to YOUR local disk
- No data is transmitted to SentinelIQ maintainers
- No telemetry, no analytics, no tracking

## NO WARRANTY

This tool identifies potential security issues based on static analysis.
It does NOT guarantee compliance. Findings must be reviewed by a qualified
security professional. Do NOT rely on SentinelIQ alone for legal/regulatory
compliance. Always validate findings against the actual standard text.
